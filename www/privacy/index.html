Privacy Policy

Silsila is an Urdu poetry app for the South Asian diaspora. We take your privacy seriously.

We collect nothing. Silsila does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts required to browse, no tracking, no analytics, and no advertisements.

Any saved poems or preferences exist only on your device. We cannot access them and they are never transmitted anywhere.

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data to share.

Silsila is appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone including children.
